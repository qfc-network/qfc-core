Engine InferenceEngine

  • CpuEngine — always available
  • MetalEngine — Apple Silicon
  • CudaEngine — NVIDIA GPU
  • OnnxEngine — ONNX Runtime
Auto-fallback: GPU fail → CPU

Scheduler ModelScheduler

  • 3-layer VRAM management
  • Hot pinned in VRAM
  • Warm LRU cached
  • Cold load on demand
  • LRU eviction when over budget

Worker InferenceWorker

  • 10s — fetch_task()
  • 30s — report_status()
  • Deadline check before inference
  • Session earnings accumulator

RPC qfc-rpc

  • qfc_getInferenceTask → assign from TaskPool
  • qfc_submitInferenceProof → verify + spot-check
  • qfc_registerMiner → MinerRegistry
  • qfc_reportMinerStatus → update models

Coordinator qfc-ai-coordinator

  • TaskPool — pending / assigned / completed
  • MinerRegistry — capabilities & tiers
  • Spot-check — 5% re-execution
  • Fee settlement — 70/10/20 split

Rewards Block Producer — distribute_rewards()

  • 60% Block Producer
  • 25% Voters (finality)
  • 15% Miner Pool — proportional to FLOPS
Fee multipliers: Embedding 1x  |  TextGen 1x  |  Whisper 2x  |  ImageGen 5x
